Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

CaveWorkers ("we", "us", or "the Service") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI workforce management platform.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up via Google authentication, we collect your email address, display name, and profile photo provided by Google.

Workspace Data: We store data you input into the Service, including company profiles, employee configurations, task requests, knowledge documents, conversation history, and approval records.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the Service, including task submissions, tool invocations, and feature usage patterns.

Tool Credentials: When you connect third-party tools, we store encrypted access tokens necessary to perform authorized actions on your behalf.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to: (a) provide, maintain, and improve the Service; (b) route tasks to appropriate AI employees; (c) process tool integrations and approvals; (d) send service notifications and billing communications; (e) detect and prevent abuse or security incidents.

4. AI Processing

The Service sends your instructions and context to third-party language model providers (e.g., Ollama-hosted models) to generate AI employee responses. We do not use your data to train third-party models. You control the instructions provided to AI employees and must approve consequential actions through our human-in-the-loop approval system.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with: (a) third-party service providers operating under contract (e.g., cloud hosting, payment processing); (b) language model providers solely to generate responses you request; (c) law enforcement when legally required.

6. Data Retention

We retain your workspace data for as long as your account is active. You may export your data or request deletion at any time through Workspace Settings. Upon account deletion, we remove your data from active systems within 30 days, with backups expiring within 90 days.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including: encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest; encryption of stored third-party tool credentials; per-workspace tenant isolation; CSRF and origin protection on all state changes; rate limiting on authentication and operations.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to: access your personal data; correct inaccurate data; delete your data; export your data in a portable format; object to or restrict processing. Contact privacy@caveworkers.com to exercise these rights.

9. Subprocessors

We engage the following categories of subprocessors: cloud infrastructure providers; payment processors (Razorpay); authentication providers (Google Firebase); language model hosting. A current list of subprocessors is available upon request.

10. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in jurisdictions where our infrastructure and subprocessors operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for any international data transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated through the Service or by email at least 30 days before taking effect.

12. Contact

For privacy inquiries or to exercise your data rights, contact us at privacy@caveworkers.com.
