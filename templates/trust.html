Security overview

Workspace records are scoped by tenant identifier throughout the application. Administrative actions use owner/admin/member RBAC. Connector credentials are encrypted before persistence, OAuth expiry and revocation are tracked, and write-capable actions can require explicit approval.

  • Firebase Authentication and Firestore-backed persistence
  • HTTPS transport through the deployment platform
  • Tenant-scoped audit events and idempotent queue jobs
  • Prompt-injection classifier before connector or web actions
  • CSRF protection, request correlation, and operational redaction

Compliance posture

Caveworkers provides account export and scheduled workspace erasure controls. Customers remain responsible for configuring their connectors, selecting lawful processing purposes, and managing their own end-user notices and retention requirements.

SOC 2: not currently certified. A formal audit is a future workstream. Caveworkers does not represent the current alpha infrastructure as SOC 2 compliant.

GDPR: export and deletion workflows are implemented in the product; legal basis, DPA execution, regional residency, and subprocessors must be reviewed for each customer deployment.

Subprocessors and connected services

The service may use the following categories depending on the tenant’s configuration:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase for hosting, identity, and Firestore
  • Configured language-model providers for AI responses
  • Google Gmail, Drive, and Sheets when a tenant grants OAuth access
  • Tenant-selected MCP servers and connector providers
  • Cloud Run or the configured deployment platform for application execution

The active subprocessor list and regional processing details should be confirmed against the deployment’s environment configuration before enterprise contracting.

Customer data controls

Workspace administrators can request a tenant-scoped JSON export. A deletion request immediately blocks operational actions, revokes locally stored connector credentials, and schedules a hard-delete cascade after the configured grace period. Audit records are anonymized during erasure where retention is required for security evidence.

Exports expire automatically. Contact support if a customer needs a formal DPA, retention schedule, or data-residency review.

Security contact

Report suspected vulnerabilities, privacy incidents, access-control issues, or provider misconfiguration to security@caveworkers.com. Do not include credentials, OAuth tokens, private keys, or confidential customer data in an initial report.
